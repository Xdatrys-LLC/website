The basics

What is Xdatrys?

Photo maps for travellers. Each map shows the top sights of a city or region, ranked by how often travellers from other countries search for them, with where to stand for the best photo or selfie and simple tips for your smartphone.

What is free?

Every map in the Map Library is free to open, with 3 spots on every map shown in full: where to stand, smartphone tips and a photo. No sign-up and no card. The Pocket Lens newsletter is free too.

What do members get?

Explorer members get every spot on every map in the Xdatrys Maps app, maps saved on the phone that work without signal, search, day tours and the best selfie spots. Pocket Scout guides and Pocket Eye albums join the membership as they are added. See Pricing.

How much does it cost?

Explorer is $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year (about $5.00 a month). Pro, with custom maps of any place you plan to visit, is coming soon at $19.99 a month or $179.99 a year. Prices are in US dollars; sales tax or VAT is shown on the payment page.

Using the maps

Do I need to download an app from an app store?

No. The Xdatrys Maps app opens in your phone's web browser. You can add it to your home screen, where it opens like any other app. The app is for members.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for maps you save. Save a map in the app while you have signal; it then works without signal, and your phone's GPS still shows where you are.

How are the spots chosen and ordered?

We list the sights travellers search for most, measured on Google searches from other countries (searches from the home country are left out), and check each spot's position against OpenStreetMap. Spot 1 is the most searched.

What does the phone icon on the map mean?

It marks where to stand, where we know the exact place from the GPS position of real photos. Where we do not, the spot's text describes where to stand instead.

A spot is wrong or a place has changed. What do I do?

Places close and change. Please email info@xdatrys.com with the map and the spot, and we will check it.

Is my location shared with Xdatrys?

No. The "You are here" dot is worked out on your phone and is not sent to us. See the Privacy policy.

My city is not in the library yet.

New maps are added every week. Tell us where you are going at info@xdatrys.com. Pro members will be able to order custom maps of any place (coming soon).

Account and payment

How do I sign in?

With your email address: we send you a one-time sign-in link, so there is no password to remember. Sign in on My account.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from My account (Manage or cancel). You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

Can I get a refund?

We do not refund the part of a period that is left when you cancel. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can ask for a full refund of your first payment within 14 days of joining. If you were charged by mistake, email us and we will put it right. See the Refund policy.

Who handles my card?

Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Can I share my membership?

Membership is for you personally; please do not share your sign-in or the member maps.

What happens to saved maps when my membership ends?

They are removed from your phone the next time the app connects to the internet.

Newsletter and contact

What is Pocket Lens?

Our free newsletter, Monday to Friday: each issue follows a real traveller to a destination, with where to stand at a photo spot, smartphone tips, a tour and a hotel, and travel news. See what is inside or subscribe.
