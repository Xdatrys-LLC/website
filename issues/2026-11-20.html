Torre dello Ziro
Amalfi

Torre dello Ziro sits above the town of Amalfi in Italy, a small elevated viewpoint roughly 10 metres across that looks out over both Amalfi and Atrani simultaneously. Most visitors never make it up here, and Best Amalfi Panoramic Views Spots: Capture Italy's Coast in 2026 specifically flags it as a less-crowded alternative to the more obvious spots on the main waterfront.

Your phone's wide-angle lens earns its keep at a viewpoint this compact. The two towns spread out in opposite directions below you, so frame horizontally and let the full width do the work. If you want to anchor the shot, the Santuario di Santa Maria del Bando sits just 80 metres away and can pull focus into the foreground.

The Basilica del Crocifisso is a 250-metre walk from the viewpoint, close enough to add a second stop without retracing much ground. Save this one for a day when you want depth in your photo set.


Map: 1 Torre dello Ziro. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Getting there: Torre dello Ziro is about 250 m northeast of Basilica del Crocifisso and 80 m south of Santuario di Santa Maria del Bando. Walk northeast from Basilica del Crocifisso to find it.

TOUR

Musement S.p.A.: Amalfi private walking tour with a local guide
Amalfi Coast, Italy

Amalfi sits at the base of Monte Cerreto, squeezed between cliff faces and the sea, and it rewards slow walking with a camera in hand. This 2.5-hour private tour, run by Musement, moves you through Saint Andrew's Cathedral, the Chiostro del Paradiso, Piazza Umberto I, and a string of quieter streets and fountains that most visitors miss entirely.


Pictured: Amalfi. Photo: ell brown, CC BY 2.0, via Flickr

Your guide covers the town's history, including the 1343 catastrophe that reshaped much of the old city, so you arrive at each spot knowing what you're actually looking at. Use the walk to clock the archways, courtyard angles, and narrow lanes on Via dei Mercanti that you'll want to return to and photograph properly. View this trip

Vista d'Amalfi
Bed And Breakfast in Amalfi, €170 per night (about US$187)

Vista d'Amalfi is a bed and breakfast at Via Matteo Camera 17, Amalfi, Italy, sitting 200 metres from the city centre and a short walk from the ferry terminal to Capri. Guests on Booking.com rate its location 9.2 out of 10, and with 1,215 reviews behind that number, it carries some weight.

THROUGH THE LENS

Phone tip: stay in one spot and let the scene shift

Slowing down is one of the most underrated moves you can make with a phone camera. Pick one spot, stay there for ten minutes, and let the scene shift around you: a boat crossing the frame, a person pausing at a wall, a reflection settling in a puddle. Your phone's tap-to-focus does the heavy lifting when you give it a still subject and a clear background.

We'd put that idea simply: patience gives you the composition that speed misses. The Hilton 2026 Trends Report found that 56% of people now travel mainly to rest and recharge, and that same unhurried mindset translates directly into stronger frames.

Pick one fixed spot today, plant your feet, and take five shots without moving your position.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Photo challenge: bridges

Today's theme: Bridges

Stand directly beneath an arch or at the base of a support pillar and point your camera straight up along the structure so the repeating curves or beams fill the frame edge to edge. Shoot in portrait orientation to stretch the geometry tall, and let the negative space of sky or water anchor the top or bottom of the shot.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
