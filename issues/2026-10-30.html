Your phone already holds your boarding pass, your hotel confirmation, and half your holiday photos , and now airports want it to carry your identity too. Wellington Temple Bar is a 3-star hotel at 23 Wellington Quay, Dublin, sitting right in the city centre near Dublin Castle, St Patrick's Cathedral, and Trinity College. THROUGH THE LENS

Many smartphones can attach location data to photo files, and some camera apps enable this by default. It's worth checking your own device: open your camera settings, look for a location or geotagging toggle, and decide whether you want it on or off before your next shot.

Try it today: the option is usually tucked inside your camera app's settings or your phone's system privacy controls, and switching it off takes about ten seconds.

Iveagh Gardens
Saint Kevin's ED, Dublin

Iveagh Gardens sits behind the National Concert Hall in Dublin's Saint Kevin's ED, and most visitors to the city walk right past it. Euro Landmarks calls it a concealed Victorian garden, with fountains, a maze, a cascade, and a sunken rose garden packed into a space about 270 metres at its longest. Visitors who find it experience that discovery firsthand.

Shoot the maze from directly above if you can get any elevation, since the geometry rewards a top-down frame on your phone. The cascade gives you moving water to work with, and the sunken rose garden drops you below the surrounding ground level, so shoot upward from the base to use that shift in height. Each of the four distinct features is a separate subject, so move between them rather than trying to fit everything into one frame.

Newman University Church sits 130 metres from the garden, and Harcourt Street Railway Station is about 210 metres away, so you can build a short neighbourhood walk around the visit. Save this one for a day when you want a quieter pocket of the city.

Getting there: Iveagh Gardens is in the Saint Kevin's ED area, about 130 m south of Newman University Church and 210 m north of Harcourt Street Railway Station. Walk south from Newman University Church to find it.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Today's theme: Markets and Stalls

Get close to the goods on the table, fill the frame with stacked colours, textures, and shapes, and let the stall itself become the background. Shoot from table height rather than standing tall, so the produce or merchandise lines up against the canopy or the crowd behind it. One tight composition beats a wide-angle sweep every time.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
