THROUGH THE LENS

Airport terminals draw more first-time Gulf visitors every year, and a lot of them arrive holding a phone with no plan for the scale they're about to face. Glass towers and wide plazas can swallow a frame and leave it feeling empty. The fix is simple: find a hard vertical edge, a column, a railing, a wall corner, and place it at the left or right third of your screen to anchor the shot.

Your phone's wide lens exaggerates depth, so anything you place close to the camera will loom large against whatever sits far behind it. Use that. A concrete bollard in the foreground, a distant spire behind it, and suddenly the frame has weight.

Today's small challenge: take one shot where a near object and a far object share the same vertical line in your frame.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Today's theme: Golden Hour

Point your lens at shadows, not the sky, long shadows stretch across pavements and walls in strong geometric lines that your phone's camera reads as high contrast. Lock your exposure by tapping the darkest part of the frame, then recompose so the shadow cuts diagonally across your shot.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
