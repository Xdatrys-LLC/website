THROUGH THE LENS

More tourists at the world's top destinations means more people in your frame. You can turn that into a strength: switch your phone to portrait mode (the setting that blurs the background so your subject stands out sharply) and let the crowd become soft colour behind whoever you are photographing. It costs nothing and takes three seconds to tap on.

Busier spots also mean faster-moving scenes, so lock your focus before you shoot. Tap the screen on the exact face or object you want sharp, hold until a small box locks onto it, then press the shutter.

Today, try tapping to lock focus on something close to you and see how the background shifts.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Today's theme: Animal Eye Contact

Get low, point your camera at the animal's eye level, and tap the screen directly on its eye to lock focus there. Hold still and wait for the animal to look straight at you before you shoot. That single moment of locked gaze turns a snapshot into a portrait.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
