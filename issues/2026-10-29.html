THROUGH THE LENS

A train window gives you something a city street rarely does: a frame that keeps moving while you stay still. Press your phone lens flat against the glass to cut the reflections, switch to your widest lens setting, and let the passing forest or coastline fill the shot.

The key is to shoot in short bursts. Most phones let you hold the shutter button to fire multiple frames fast, so you pick the sharpest one after.

Try it on your next train leg: press the lens to the glass, hold the shutter for three seconds, and choose your favourite frame at the other end.

HOT LOCATION


Photo: Vanja Milicic, Unsplash

Chidorigafuchi Moat
Tokyo

Chidorigafuchi Moat runs for about 710 metres alongside the defences of Tokyo's Imperial Palace, and Photo spots in Tokyo calls out its stone walls, water reflections, and seasonal trees as the raw material for strong compositions. The moat offers views toward the Imperial Palace defenses, and its generous length means you have plenty of room to move and reframe without feeling hemmed in.

Point your phone down toward the water and let the stone walls fill one edge of the frame, so the reflection doubles the depth of the shot. The moat's length gives you room to walk and reframe repeatedly, finding the angle where the trees break the skyline above the wall. Try a square crop to lock the reflection and the wall into equal halves.

Inuimon Gate sits about 400 metres away, so once you've worked the moat, walk over and compare the two scales: the wide open water versus the more compact stonework of the gate.

Getting there: Chidorigafuchi Moat is about 390 m southeast of Manulife Place kudan Minami and 400 m west of Inuimon Gate. Walk southeast from Manulife Place kudan Minami to find it.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Today's theme: Street Portrait

Get close enough that your subject fills at least half the frame, then tap their face on your phone screen to lock focus there. A plain wall, a shop shutter, or a single colour behind them keeps the eye where you want it. No cluttered background, no wandering gaze.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
