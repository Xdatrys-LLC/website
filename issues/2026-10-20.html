Antek & Tomek: Local Free Tour with Antek & Tomek | Old Town & More | small groups
October 19, 2026, Warsaw, Poland

Antek & Tomek run a free walking tour of Warsaw's Old Town and surrounding streets, bookable from 19 October 2026, with dates running across the week. The tour lasts two hours and keeps group sizes small, so you get actual room to stop, look around, and think about a frame rather than shuffling along in a crowd.

THROUGH THE LENS

A closed museum door is a gift you didn't ask for. Your whole shooting plan just opened up, and the street outside is full of people reacting to the same news you are.

Keep your phone's Portrait mode off for this kind of scene. Portrait mode blurs the background artificially, and a crowd or a locked gate reads better when everything stays sharp and in context.

Try this today: photograph one flat, closed door from straight on, filling the frame with its texture and colour.

HOT LOCATION


Photo: Julia Filirovska, Pexels

Most Gdański
Praga-Północ, Warsaw

Most Gdański in Warsaw's Praga-Północ district carries trams on its upper deck and road traffic below, a two-level steel-and-wood structure that Best Photo Spots in Warsaw calls out specifically for its modernist staircase and views toward the Warsaw Zoo. The bridge sits on a one-way street 1,390 metres long with a 50 km/h speed limit, so you're photographing against moving trams and vehicles, not a static scene.

Face the staircase and shoot vertical to fill the frame with its geometry from top to bottom. From the upper deck, line up the tram tracks as a leading line pointing toward the zoo side of the river, and get close to the railing so the two-level structure reads clearly in the background.

Two bird rehabilitation centres sit within 130 metres of the bridge, so once you've got your shots, a short walk puts you near the work of Ptasi Azyl and Ptasi Punkt Przyjęć. Save this for later if you ever find yourself on the Praga side of the Vistula with a spare hour.

Getting there: Most Gdański is in the Praga-Północ area, about 110 m north of Ptasi Punkt Przyjęć and 130 m north of Ośrodek Rehabilitacji Krajowych Ptaków Chronionych "Ptasi Azyl". It meets Stefana Starzyńskiego. Walk north from Ptasi Punkt Przyjęć to find it. It is a public street and free to visit.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Today's theme: Food and Texture

Get your phone right down close to your subject, so the rough crust of bread, the grainy dusting of spice, or the glossy pull of melted cheese fills the whole frame. Tap to focus on the sharpest edge of the texture, then keep your background plain and dark so nothing steals attention from the surface detail.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
