How to correct a map ("correct" link):
  1. Tap a pin. Its phone (stand-here point) and the spot's panel appear.
  2. Zoom in close. Drag the magenta pin onto the subject and the phone onto where to stand (drag the dashed box around each).
  3. "Swap pin and phone" fixes a reversed pair in one tap. Type anything else (text, photo, name) in the note box.
  4. When finished, press Copy corrections and paste the text into the chat with Claude. Your changes stay in this browser until you press "Clear all".
Flagged spots (things the automatic checks want you to look at) say so in yellow in the panel.
