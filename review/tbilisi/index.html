About this map

This map shows 18 of the most-searched sights in Tbilisi as photo spots, ranked by how often people search for them: Narikala Fortress, Metekhi Church, Freedom Square, Mtatsminda Park and Sameba Cathedral lead the list. Every spot has a pin on the subject and a phone icon showing where to stand, with tips for shooting it on your phone: which lens to use, how to frame it, a settings tip and the best time of day. Directions and Navigate buttons take you to the standing point on foot or by public transport. 3 spots are free to view; the full map is for members.

The first 3:

  1. Narikala Fortress: Narikala is an old hilltop fortress above Tbilisi, looking down on the Mtkvari (Kura) River.
  2. Metekhi Church: Metekhi Church stands right on the edge of a cliff above the Kura river, looking across to the Old Town.
  3. Freedom Square, Tbilisi: Freedom Square sits at the eastern end of Rustaveli Avenue, the main street of Tbilisi, and the gilded figure of St George slaying the dragon stands in the middle of it.

Questions

What are the top photo spots in Tbilisi?

The first three on this map are Narikala Fortress, Metekhi Church and Freedom Square. They are ranked by how often people search for them. The full map has 18.

Where should I stand to photograph Narikala Fortress?

Stand on Metekhi Bridge (middle of the bridge, looking upstream toward the fortress). Start with the default lens (1x) for the walls and the church.

When is the best time to photograph Metekhi Church?

Late afternoon or sunset for warm light. Early morning is quieter.

How do I get to Freedom Square?

Start from the centre of Tbilisi and head for Freedom Square at the eastern end of Rustaveli Avenue.
