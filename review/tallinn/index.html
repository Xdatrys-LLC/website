About this map

This map shows 17 of the most-searched sights in Tallinn as photo spots, ranked by how often people search for them: Toompea Castle, Seaplane Harbour, Kadriorg Palace, Viru Gate and Telliskivi lead the list. Every spot has a pin on the subject and a phone icon showing where to stand, with tips for shooting it on your phone: which lens to use, how to frame it, a settings tip and the best time of day. Directions and Navigate buttons take you to the standing point on foot or by public transport. 3 spots are free to view; the full map is for members.

The first 3:

  1. Toompea Castle: Toompea Castle sits on top of Toompea hill in central Tallinn, and today it is home to the Parliament of Estonia, the Riigikogu.
  2. Seaplane Harbour: Seaplane Harbour (Lennusadam) is a maritime museum that opened in spring 2012, set in a building first put up as a hangar for seaplanes.
  3. Kadriorg Palace: Kadriorg Palace is a pale Baroque building that Peter the Great had built for his wife Catherine.

Questions

What are the top photo spots in Tallinn?

The first three on this map are Toompea Castle, Seaplane Harbour and Kadriorg Palace. They are ranked by how often people search for them. The full map has 17.

Where should I stand to photograph Toompea Castle?

Stand about 20 m east of Toompea Castle, at the phone icon on the map, and face west. Start with the default lens (1x) for the whole façade, then switch to the ultra-wide (0.5x) if you are too close to fit the wing in.

When is the best time to photograph Seaplane Harbour?

Soft light in the early morning or late afternoon usually flatters the hangar and the water, but the spot works in most daylight.

How do I get to Kadriorg Palace?

No station or tram stop is listed for this spot.
