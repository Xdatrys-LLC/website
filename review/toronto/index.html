About this map

This map shows 20 of the most-searched sights in Toronto as photo spots, ranked by how often people search for them: CN Tower, Casa Loma, Royal Ontario Museum, St. Lawrence Market and Hockey Hall of Fame lead the list. Every spot has a pin on the subject and a phone icon showing where to stand, with tips for shooting it on your phone: which lens to use, how to frame it, a settings tip and the best time of day. Directions and Navigate buttons take you to the standing point on foot or by public transport. The first 3 spots are free to view; the full map is for members.

The first 3:

  1. CN Tower: The CN Tower is Toronto's signature landmark, a 553.3-metre communications and observation tower that was completed in 1976.
  2. Casa Loma: Casa Loma is a Gothic Revival, castle-style mansion on a hill in midtown Toronto.
  3. Royal Ontario Museum (the Crystal): The Royal Ontario Museum is one of the largest museums in North America, and its Michael Lee-Chin Crystal is the part that stops people on the pavement.

Questions

What are the top photo spots in Toronto?

The first three on this map are CN Tower, Casa Loma and Royal Ontario Museum. They are ranked by how often people search for them. The full map has 20.

Where should I stand to photograph the CN Tower?

Stand about 190 m south-east of the CN Tower, in Roundhouse Park (the phone icon on the map), and face north-west. Start with the ultra-wide (0.5x) to fit the whole tower in one frame, then try the default lens (1x) for a cleaner look with less stretching at the edges.

When is the best time to photograph Casa Loma?

Try mid to late afternoon, when the front of the castle may catch softer light. Trees along the street can screen the building when in leaf, so you may get a clearer view of the stonework in late autumn, winter or early spring.

How do I get to the Royal Ontario Museum?

Take the Line 1 subway to Museum station, whose north-western entrance serves the museum directly.
