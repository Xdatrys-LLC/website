How to use this map

Find the shot, stand in the right place, and take a photo (or a selfie) you'll love.

1
Tap a pink pinEach pin is a famous sight. The card shows a photo, where to stand and simple phone tips.
2
Walk to the blue phoneThe blue phone icon is the exact spot to stand. Tap "Walk there" and your maps app takes you.
3
Shoot it, then grab a selfieFollow the zoom and timing tips. The pink smiley marks the best selfie spot.
📍
See where you areTap the target button (top right) and allow location: a blue dot shows you on the map and moves as you walk. It stays on your phone.
4
Want a plan? Turn on the Day tourMenu ☰ → Day tour shows a route to every spot, split into days, with step-by-step directions.
📲 Keep it on your phone

Install the map as an app: it opens full screen from your home screen.
