📲 Put Xdatrys Maps on your phone

Install it once: it opens full screen from your home screen, and every map you save works without signal.

1Tap the Share button ⎙ (bottom of Safari, or top right in Chrome)
2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
3Tap Add. Then open Xdatrys Maps from your home screen
