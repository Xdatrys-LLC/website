Explorer membership gives you access to every spot on every map in the Xdatrys Map Library through the Xdatrys Maps app (a web app you open in your phone's browser and can add to your home screen), for as long as your membership is active. New maps are added over time; maps may also be changed, corrected or withdrawn. Guides and albums are added to membership as they become available.

2. Payment and renewal

Membership is paid in advance, monthly or yearly, and renews automatically at the end of each period at the price shown when you joined, until you cancel. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not see or store your card details. Prices include or exclude sales tax/VAT as shown on the payment page. If we change the price, we will email you at least 30 days before it applies to your next renewal.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel any time from My account (Manage or cancel). Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Refunds are described in the Refund policy.

4. Your account

You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email address. Membership is for you personally: please do not share your sign-in or the member maps. Maps saved on your phone keep working without signal; when your membership has ended, they are removed the next time the app connects.

5. Using the maps safely and lawfully

The maps suggest where to stand to take a photo. Always follow local laws, signs, opening hours and site rules (some places ban photography, drones or tripods), respect private property and other people, and look after your own safety: watch for traffic, edges, water and weather. Directions and walking times are estimates; places close or change. You use the maps at your own risk.
