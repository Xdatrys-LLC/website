    How it works

    Three steps to the shot

    Open a map on your phone before you go, or once you arrive.

    1. 1

      Find the spot

      Open the map for your city. Numbered magenta pins show the photo subjects, starting with the most searched.

    2. 2

      Stand at the phone icon

      The cyan phone shows where to stand, and the arrow shows which way to face. Tap Navigate to walk there with Google Maps or Apple Maps.

    3. 3

      Shoot with the tips

      Every spot has a "Shooting it on your phone" section: which lens, how to frame it, a settings tip and the best time to go.
