Privacy notice

What we collect, why, who handles it and how to take it back. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who we are

Xdatrys LLC (“Xdatrys”, “we”) runs this website and the Pocket Lens newsletter. We decide how the information described here is used. You can reach us at info@xdatrys.com or by post at Xdatrys LLC, 44 Rue Pasquier, 75008 Paris, France.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you subscribe to Pocket Lens through a form on this site or on our Pocket Lens page. This is the only personal information we ask for on this site.
  • Purchase details, if you buy a guide. Gumroad takes the payment and holds those details. We receive your name, email address and what you bought.
  • Basic visit data. Our website host records technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
  • Messages you send us, for example by email.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To send you the Pocket Lens newsletter. We do this because you asked us to, which means your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To deliver a download you requested, such as a shot map.
  • To process and support a purchase. We do this to fulfil the contract with you.
  • To keep the site secure and working. We do this in our legitimate interest.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it to profile you or to make automated decisions about you.

Who else handles it

We use these services. Each has its own privacy policy and handles your information on our behalf or on its own account:

  • beehiiv stores subscribers and sends the newsletter. When you subscribe, beehiiv emails you to confirm your address.
  • Gumroad sells and delivers our guides and albums.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site. Your browser fetches them from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • GitHub serves some images on this site, with the same effect.

These companies may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards for those transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a minimal record that you unsubscribed so that we do not email you again. Purchase records are kept as long as the law and our accounts require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit or object to how we use it, or give it to you in a portable form. You can withdraw consent at any time. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, or you can email us. We will reply within one month.

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you can also complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. The subscribe form and links to services such as beehiiv and Gumroad may set cookies when you use them. Please read their policies for details.

Children

Xdatrys is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you think a child has given us their email address, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page if we change how we handle your information, and we will change the date at the top.
