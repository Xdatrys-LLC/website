Dresden: where to stand

Better photos start before you shoot. Three places to stand for the Frauenkirche, and a taste of the full Pocket Scout guide.

Most maps pin the thing you want to photograph. This one shows where to stand. Position is accurate to about 10 metres, so look around you before you raise your phone.

Where to stand

  1. 1Stand 120 m back, early morning. Frame the dome with branches.
  2. 2Crouch low by the stone steps and shoot across the cobblestones.
  3. 3Step back to the square's edge for one wide shot of the church.
