HOT LOCATION

Castello Baradello, San Rocco, Como: where to stand for the photo


Photo: Ottantafame, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Castello Baradello
San Rocco, Como

Castello Baradello in Como's San Rocco area is the kind of place most visitors skip entirely. "Como's Best Photo Spots: Capture Stunning Lake Como Memories" calls it an often-overlooked medieval castle that gives you an elevated perspective over Como city and the first basin of Lake Como. That elevated position is the whole reason to go.

Your phone's wide-angle lens earns its keep here: shoot down across the city rooflines toward the lake basin, letting the cityscape fill the lower frame while the water anchors the background. A change of direction works too, so turn and frame the castle itself from below to show scale against the sky. Neither shot requires anything fancy, just a steady hand and a deliberate composition choice.

Basilica di San Carpoforo sits 360 metres from the castle, and Oratorio di Santa Brigida is 310 metres away, so a short walk connects all three. We'd suggest treating them as one loop rather than three separate stops.


Map: 1 Castello Baradello. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Getting there: Castello Baradello is in the San Rocco area, about 360 m west of Basilica di San Carpoforo and 310 m north of Oratorio di Santa Brigida. Walk west from Basilica di San Carpoforo to find it.

TOUR

RomaeTravel: Como Walking Tour
Lake Como, Como, Italy, Milan

RomaeTravel runs this two-hour guided walk through Como's historic centre, taking in Piazza Cavour, the Cathedral of Como, and the lakeside promenade with a licensed local guide in a small group. At €60 per person, you can cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund, and booking goes straight to the operator.

Caesar Suites by "The House Of Travelers"
Guest House in Como, €140 per night (about US$153)

Caesar Suites by "The House Of Travelers" is a guest house at 29 Via Cesare Cantù in Como's city centre, with a private bathroom, terrace, air conditioning, and free WiFi in every room. Guests on Booking.com rate its location 9.3 out of 10, and the Basilica of San Fedele sits 300 metres away.

THROUGH THE LENS

Phone tip: photograph one small detail so tightly nothing else fits

Crowds that used to thin out in autumn now stay thick well into winter, according to the European Travel Commission's November 2026 findings. That's worth knowing before you raise your phone, because a packed promenade can ruin an otherwise strong shot.

The fix is simple: get closer. Filling your frame with one person, one wooden shutter, or the green water lapping a stone jetty pushes the crowd out of the picture entirely. Your phone's portrait mode helps here too, softening everything behind your main subject so the background noise disappears.

Try it today: pick one small detail at arm's length and photograph it so tightly that nothing else fits in the frame.

FRAME THE MOMENT

Photo challenge: signs and lettering

Today's theme: Signs and Lettering

Point your phone's camera straight at a sign, then step back and include the wall, door, or surface it sits on so the letters sit inside a wider frame. Look for contrast between the font's colour and the background behind it, and try shooting at a slight angle to add depth to flat lettering.

Shoot it anywhere in the world. The theme is all that matters.
